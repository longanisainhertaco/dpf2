# Magnetohydrodynamics

Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) treats plasma as a single conducting fluid. The DPF2 MHD solver evolves mass, momentum, energy, and magnetic field with resistive terms and source terms for radiation and fusion reactions.

## Key Equations

- Continuity equation for mass conservation
- Momentum equation with Lorentz force and pressure gradients
- Induction equation including resistivity
- Energy equation capturing Ohmic and radiation losses

These equations underpin sheath dynamics and pinch behavior in dense plasma focus devices.
